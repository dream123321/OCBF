+# DCBF Update - 2026-09-05

## 中文

- 新增低磁盘训练数据布局。逐轮 DFT 成功结构以普通未压缩 XYZ 分片保存在外置 `summary_bundle/datasets/training_history/xyz/`，并由 manifest 记录来源、main/gen、结构数和 SHA-256。最终 `all.xyz` 从这些分片按固定顺序生成，便于逐轮追溯。
- 主动学习运行期间只维护 `workspace/.dcbf_runtime/training/train.cfg`。采样训练、候选触发统计和默认高精度训练共用该文件；完整流程成功后删除，失败或中断时保留并可由 XYZ 历史分片重建。用户显式指定另一份高精度训练输入时仍保留原转换行为。
- DFT 成功任务默认按任务归档到 `summary_bundle/raw_dft/<engine>/...`。VASP、ABACUS、QE 和 CP2K 均保留结构与包含 E/F/S 的主输出，并在输入明确请求时一并保存电荷密度。未请求或请求后缺失会写入警告和 manifest；归档校验失败时不删除原始文件。
- 相对路径 `summary.output_dir` 现在从 `workspace` 的父目录解析，因此默认 summary 位于运行根目录而不在 workspace 内。数据集、日志、模型、来源文件和 DFT 归档可独立保留，workspace 可在流程结束后清理。
- Coverage summary 只导出最终 PCA 图、`coverage_summary.csv`、`coverage_remark.txt`、`query_manifest.json` 和压缩后的 `query.xyz.gz`。完成并写入 summary manifest 后，workspace 中可重建的 descriptor、split XYZ、PCA 文本和 query 运行目录会被清理。
- 本版不修改采样、覆盖率、筛选、DFT 标签收集和训练算法，也未重新编译 SUS2、LAMMPS 或 PLUMED。

## English

- Added a low-disk training-data layout. Successful DFT structures from each generation are retained as ordinary uncompressed XYZ shards under `summary_bundle/datasets/training_history/xyz/`, with source, main/gen, structure count, and SHA-256 recorded in a manifest. The final `all.xyz` is assembled from those shards in a fixed order for generation-level traceability.
- Active learning now maintains only `workspace/.dcbf_runtime/training/train.cfg`. Sampling training, candidate-trigger counting, and the default final high-precision training share this file. It is removed only after the complete workflow succeeds, retained after failure or interruption, and can be rebuilt from the XYZ history. Explicitly configured alternative high-precision training inputs retain the previous conversion behavior.
- Successful DFT tasks are archived per task under `summary_bundle/raw_dft/<engine>/...`. VASP, ABACUS, QE, and CP2K archives retain the structure and primary E/F/S output, plus charge-density files when explicitly requested by the input. Missing or unrequested charge output is recorded as a warning and in the manifest; original files are never removed when archive verification fails.
- Relative `summary.output_dir` paths are now resolved from the parent of `workspace`, so the default summary sits at the run root instead of inside the workspace. Datasets, logs, models, source inputs, and raw DFT archives can therefore be retained independently while the completed workspace is cleaned.
- Coverage summary export is limited to final PCA figures, `coverage_summary.csv`, `coverage_remark.txt`, `query_manifest.json`, and compressed `query.xyz.gz`. Rebuildable descriptor, split-XYZ, PCA-text, and query-run intermediates are removed from the workspace only after the summary manifest is successfully published.
- This release does not change sampling, coverage, selection, DFT label collection, or training algorithms, and does not rebuild SUS2, LAMMPS, or PLUMED.
